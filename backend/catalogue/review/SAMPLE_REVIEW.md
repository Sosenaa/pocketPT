## Findings

- Milk: leave unresolved until a suitable UK semi-skimmed reference or a specific product with a valid gram-based conversion is available. No US 2% substitute has been assigned.
- Peanut butter: leave unresolved until the source confirms peanuts-only formulation. Generic peanut-butter nutrition is not assumed to describe it.
- Lentils: leave unresolved until canned, drained nutrition is established. Dry and boiled records were not substituted.
- Cheese: the cottage-cheese reference is explicitly creamed (4.3g fat/100g). Vegetarian/vegan product suitability is not established by its nutrition record.
- Bread: a generic commercial whole-wheat reference is used for nutrition. An actual UK product can differ; suitability and allergen checks remain pending.
- Salmon: the reference is explicitly Atlantic farmed salmon. Its name was made more specific in the shared food catalogue.
- Food weights are consistent with the reviewed states: raw chicken/fish/vegetables, dry rice/quinoa/oats, drained canned pulses, edible portions and gram-weighted liquids.
- Meals use varied base portions. No portion changes were made during this source review, and no claim is made that any meal or combination meets a person's targets.
- Catalogue ingredients with null adjustment bounds cannot yet be scaled independently by an automatic portion optimiser.
